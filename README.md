🧬 Graph-Based Federated AI for Identifying Protein Targets for Mutant p53 Reactivation
📊 TP53 Protein Interaction Dashboard

This project presents an interactive dashboard for exploring protein-protein interaction (PPI) networks related to TP53 (p53) — a critical tumor suppressor gene widely studied in cancer research.

TP53 mutations are strongly associated with multiple cancers and understanding its interaction network is essential for identifying potential therapeutic targets.

🚀 Live Demo

🔗 Dashboard:
https://aashishcodegithub.github.io/Graph-Based-Federated-AI-for-Identifying-Protein-Targets-for-Mutant-p53-Reactivation-/ppi/tp53_dashboard.html

🎯 Objective
Analyze TP53-centered protein interaction networks
Identify key interacting proteins that could act as drug targets
Leverage Graph-based AI + Federated Learning concepts
Provide an interactive visualization for better understanding
🧠 Key Features
📌 Interactive PPI Network Visualization
🔍 Node-level exploration of protein interactions
📈 Graph-based insights into biological relationships
⚡ Lightweight web-based dashboard (no installation required)
🧬 About TP53
TP53 is known as the "guardian of the genome"
Plays a major role in:
Cell cycle regulation
DNA repair
Apoptosis
Mutations in TP53 are among the most common in human cancers
